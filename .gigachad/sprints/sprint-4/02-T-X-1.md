## Description

Fix lifecycle robustness findings #0–#4: escalate kills per process group, persist supervisor pid under the job lock, reconcile from result.json, clean up the worker on supervisor exceptions, and skip incomplete job directories.

## Plan

1. **#0** Kill helper: after SIGTERM to the group, wait the grace period while *any* process in the group is alive
   (probe with `os.killpg(pgid, 0)`; ProcessLookupError = group gone), then SIGKILL the group — independent of the
   leader's liveness. Use it everywhere (timeout, cancel, supervisor-died finalization).
2. **#1** `start()`: spawn the supervisor and persist `supervisor_pid` to meta.json under the job lock before returning;
   the supervisor's claim step must take the same lock (so no lost update).
3. **#2** Reconciliation: under the lock, if result.json exists and meta is non-terminal, copy the terminal fields from
   result.json into meta and save; return the terminal job. Only if neither is terminal and the supervisor is dead,
   finalize `failed` / `supervisor died` (with group kill from #0).
4. **#3** Supervisor: wrap everything after Popen in try/except/finally that, on exception, kills the worker group
   (#0 helper), reaps it, and records `failed` with the exception text. Test-only hook
   `GIGACHAD_LITE_TEST_FAIL_AFTER_SPAWN` raises right after spawn (document as test-only next to the existing delay hook).
5. **#4** `list()` skips directories without meta.json and tolerates meta disappearing (FileNotFoundError /
   JSONDecodeError) between enumeration and load. `_resolve`/`get` treat a dir without meta.json as unknown (KeyError → CLI exit 2).
6. Remove `red_phase` markers once green. Run `pytest tests/test_jobs.py` 3× locally to confirm no flakiness.
